Just These Buttons

The blueprint for the PRD: a web page, one small server route that keeps the key secret, and Gemini 3.8 Flash reading the photo. No database, no login. The photo is never saved.

How this works, in plain language

The web page

Takes the photo, shrinks it, shows each stage, draws the dimming and numbers, handles taps, lays out the printout.

The server route

Adds the secret key, asks Gemini, checks the answer is well-formed, sends back at most five steps.

Gemini 3.8 Flash

Looks at the photo and returns a box around each needed control, what's printed on it, and a plain instruction.

The 0–1000 grid

Gemini describes where a control is with four numbers, on a grid that runs 0 to 1000 across and down the photo, whatever its real size:

"box_2d": [412, 88, 520, 160]
          top  left bottom right

The overlay draws in that same 0–1000 space and is stretched over the photo, so the model's numbers land on the photo with no conversion step to go wrong. A tap on the photo is turned into the same grid, which is how the app knows which button was tapped.

App shell and stages JustTheseButtons.tsx

Client component holding all state in one reducer; renders the stages in order. prd.md › The Core Journey · Screens and Layout

Photo stage PhotoStage.tsx · lib/image.ts

Promise, photo button, sample link, privacy line, photo with Retake. Decode failure → “photo can't be used”. prd.md › Taking the photo

Task stage TaskStage.tsx

Labelled field, 120 characters, Find the buttons disabled until photo + text. prd.md › Naming the task

Find-buttons route route.ts · gemini.ts

Validate (zod) → 413 over 4 MB → rate limit (20 per 10 min per IP, daily cap) → Gemini with 25 s timeout → validate, clamp, sort, cap five, map low confidence to “check this one”. Logs status, count and duration only. prd.md › Finding the buttons · States and Boundaries

Prompt lib/prompt.ts

Fewest controls for an elderly person, in order, two or three and never over five; box the physical control not its label; for a dial, name the setting printed on the machine; short plain wording that quotes the printed text; flag low confidence; say when the photo or task won't work.

Button overlay ButtonOverlay.tsx

SVG in a 0–1000 viewBox over the photo's exact aspect ratio. Dimmed image, original through a mask of rounded boxes, rings and badges. printMode switches to black and white for the print sheet.

Step list and editing StepList.tsx · lib/steps.ts

Editable instruction, move up/down, flag tag and “looks right”. Editing a flagged step clears its flag. A tapped-in step gets a default box about 7% of the photo's width, reading “Press this button”.

Print sheet PrintSheet.tsx

Print-only. 186 × 250 mm content box. Card: task as title, overlay, numbered steps. Sticker strip below a dashed cut line.

Sample machine lib/samples.ts · public/samples/

Three real, openly licensed photos from Wikimedia Commons — washing machine, microwave, TV remote — each with a task and its credit, shown under the photo and printed on the card. Same image path, real route; the kernel is never faked.

Status messages StatusMessage.tsx

The working line and the four error states with the exact PRD copy and their actions.

Vision probe scripts/probe.ts

Developer tool: runs the same findButtons() over probe/cases.json and writes a contact sheet to judge by eye. Summary goes to docs/vision-probe.md.

Where it will break, and what happens then

Box on the wrong control

Most likely on remotes. The caregiver corrects it before printing, and low-confidence steps arrive flagged. The probe measures how often it happens.

Gemini slow or down

25 s timeout → “Something went wrong finding the buttons. Your photo is still here — try again.”

Photo won't decode

“Photo can't be used” message with Retake.

Printout spills over

Fixed-millimetre layout sized to the smaller of A4 and Letter; checked in print preview.

The useful unknown

How far can the model's coordinates be trusted? This is your learning goal. The first build step runs the vision probe on at least 10 photos — washing machine, TV remote, air-conditioner remote, microwave, rice cooker — and records the hit rate in docs/vision-probe.md. They start as Gemini-generated photos (your choice); generated photos are cleaner than real ones, so real photos from home are added before the demo is recorded and reported separately.

≥ 8 of 10 correctEvery needed control is inside its ring → proceed as planned.
FewerCompare another model through GEMINI_MODEL, tighten the prompt, and lean harder on flagging and correction in the demo.
